## Reading the receipts

- Fresh-slot premium as paid on the ring: lock A first use 160,410 against reuse 62,504, 97,906 gas. The legacy vault pays it on every lock (first 159,692, reuse 159,692: no reuse to speak of).
- Steady-state `lock`: ring 62,504 against legacy 159,692, 97,188 gas less per swap.
- `claimBySig` (warm, round first): ring 93,949 against legacy 81,841, 12,108 gas more: the ring writes `CONSUMED` (non-zero to non-zero) where the legacy vault deletes the slot and earns the clearing refund, and it carries one more calldata word and one more event field. The same delta shows on the cold claim and, without any transfer, on `refund` (44,292 against 35,037).
- Net per happy-path swap, warm: 85,080 gas less on the ring (156,453 against 241,533).
- Reusing an expired, unconsumed reservation: the ring's lock E rewrites the slot and releases it inline in 54,183 gas; the legacy vault needs `refund` 35,047 plus `lock` 159,692 = 194,739, two transactions.
- The escape hatch is unchanged in kind: the sponsored `claimSelf` costs the EntryPoint `actualGasUsed` 343,429 on the ring against 342,684 on the legacy vault; the claimant held zero ETH before and after in both, with no prerequisite transaction (authorization nonce 0 in both).
- Floor: a bare USDT transfer is 54,497 warm / 152,417 cold; the cold column is the recipient's fresh balance slot in the token, the same on both vaults and on any design.

## What did not run

- The run stopped at: `transaction reverted: 0xbdd8e2199e40f2543cefcd669cd5d65454c23091514d5f21f803639e9fd2ea6a`.
- Round-reuse receipts not taken: legacy claimBySig A, legacy claimBySig B, legacy sponsored claimSelf D, legacy refund C, ring claimBySig A, ring claimBySig B, ring sponsored claimSelf D, ring refund C. Their round-first counterparts stand in above.
- Why it stopped: the legacy `claimBySig` A of round reuse reverted `ReservationExpired`. Its `lock` had waited 12 minutes for a receipt on the public RPC and the following nine locks another 17, so the 1,800 s expiry set at send time had passed. The script now sets 7,200 s for the claimed shapes.
- Why it was not repeated: from 2026-09-29 23:00 UTC the public RPC (`rpc.plataberget.ethpandaops.io`) answered 503 or held receipts for over an hour while the devnet kept producing blocks (dora, 2026-09-30 12:03 UTC: slots proposing, participation 50%); a rerun started 22:55 UTC stalled for eight hours on one lock and was stopped. No per-node endpoint is published. The run is repeatable with `npm run measure:glam` once the RPC answers.
- Nine earlier attempts on 2026-09-29 failed in setup or in round first for reasons that were fixed in the script each time: expiry computed before slow sends, read-backs pinned to a block on a lagging node (state assertions now use the receipts' events), a node rejecting `eth_call` state overrides (preflight is skipped there), a node without state reverting every `eth_estimateGas` (retried, then a fixed limit). Their receipts are on dora under the same two vault addresses; the ring indices 0..44 they used were left live or consumed and this run used 50..54.
